Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

Google Analytics

SAR11 Genome Atlas uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how the website is used and to improve its content and usability. Analytics is optional and is loaded only after you select “Accept analytics” in the consent banner.

When enabled, Google may receive information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser information, and interactions with the site. This information is processed by Google under its applicable privacy terms and may be processed outside your country.

Data retention

Analytics data is retained according to the Google Analytics property settings. Aggregated reports may be retained separately from user-level and event-level data. See Google Analytics’ current data-retention documentation for the applicable period.

Your choices

You can decline Analytics when the banner appears. To change your choice later, select “Privacy settings” at the bottom of any page and choose Decline or Accept analytics. Declining or withdrawing consent stops future Analytics loading on this site; it does not delete data already processed by Google.

Contact

For questions about this site or this policy, please use the Contact page. For more information about how Google Analytics handles data, please also see Google’s Safeguarding your data and Privacy Policy.
