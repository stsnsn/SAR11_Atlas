Data access

Download

Access the genome metadata and analysis resources underlying the SAR11 Genome Atlas.

Compact, web-facing release files can be downloaded directly below. Larger reproducibility archives, including sequences, complete annotations, analysis outputs, and HMM profiles, will be released through Zenodo.

Species phylogenies

Species trees inferred from bac120 and from SAR11_165 HMM profiles obtained from the Meren Lab workflow. SAR11_165 is a SAR11-focused subset of an earlier 200-gene Alphaproteobacterial SCG collection. The rooted IQ-TREE phylogeny was pruned to 542 SAR11 genomes after rooting with 20 alphaproteobacterial outgroups.
