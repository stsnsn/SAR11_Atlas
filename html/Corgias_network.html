CORGIAS Network


This is an undirected network showing co-occurrence or anti-occurrence between orthogroups (OGs).
Nodes: Represent OGs, colored by their COG functional categories.
Edges: Weighted by the statistical significance, calculated as $-\log_{10}(q\text{-value})$. Thicker edges represent more significant associations.
Colors: Red indicates positive correlation (co-occurrence), while Blue indicates negative correlation (anti-occurrence).

How to use: Enter an OG ID (e.g., OG0000123) and click the Extract and show button.
