BLAST Search (developing)


On this page, you can identify which ortholog group your amino acid sequence most closely matches using a simple Smith–Waterman alignment. For more comprehensive analyses, you can also access the full BLAST database on the Download page.

今は仮でロドプシン配列たちに検索がかかるようになっています。多少汚い（スペースや数字が混ざっている）FASTAを投げても大丈夫です。

Currently, the search is configured to use a preliminary dataset of rhodopsin sequences. The analysis runs entirely in your browser, so its speed depends on your device’s processing performance. You can also input slightly unclean FASTA files (e.g., those containing spaces or numbers) — they will still be processed correctly.

inputの例：
>query
NIESLFEYTVGQFEAIDHLLTMGVGAHLAALVFFILVSRFVAPKYRVATALSCIVMVSAGQATMWTDAFAFNGRVYEPTDLTFSNGVNWMVTIPCLLTQLLIVLNIRGAALFKTAAALIFFAWGMIITGYVGQLYEVESIDQ
