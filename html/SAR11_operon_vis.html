Genome Context Viewer (Developing)


This page provides an interactive visualization tool for exploring the gene structures of SAR11 operons. Users can input a specific Orthologous Group (OG) to view the corresponding genes, their positions, and orientations. Key features include:

  • Top 20 OG Highlighting: Highlights the most frequent 20 OGs with distinct colors, while others are displayed in gray.
  • Interactive Tooltips: Hover over genes to see detailed information such as sequence ID, group ID, and functional annotations.

This tool is designed to help researchers and enthusiasts better understand the genomic context and relationships within the SAR11 clade.

Citation information:

Nishino et al. "SAR11 Genome Atlas." ongoing.

Nishino et al. "Functional Unknomics of the SAR11 clade." in prep.
