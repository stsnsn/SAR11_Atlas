Structural exploration

Predicted Protein Structures

Find UniProt matches for SAR11 proteins and inspect available AlphaFoldDB predictions in an interactive 3D viewer.

Protein sequences were matched to UniProt (Jan 2026) with MMseqs2 v13.45111 using a minimum sequence identity of 0.85 and up to one match per query. A match below 100% identity is not sequence-identical to the SAR11 protein, and not every accession has an AlphaFoldDB prediction.
