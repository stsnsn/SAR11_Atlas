Predicted Protein Structures

Find UniProt matches for SAR11 proteins and inspect available AlphaFoldDB predictions in an interactive 3D viewer.

The table lists 3,660 SAR11 protein matches with confirmed AlphaFoldDB models across 1,711 orthogroups. A match below 100% identity is not sequence-identical to the SAR11 protein.

Search or filter the table by orthogroup, gene, genome, or annotation. Select an accession to open its AlphaFoldDB structure in the viewer below; select an orthogroup to explore related SAR11 information.

Complete sequence-similarity search results against UniProtKB 2026_01 Swiss-Prot and TrEMBL are available from the Download page.

SAR11 protein matches in UniProt

Search and filter the embedded table, then use an accession in the structure viewer below.

Open the match table in full screen

How this table was prepared: All 542-genome protein sequences were searched against UniProtKB release 2026_01 with DIAMOND v2.1.10.164. Hits with at least 85% sequence identity were retained, and up to the five highest-ranking hits per orthogroup were screened against the AlphaFold DB prediction API. The table therefore lists, at most, the top five hits for each orthogroup when a predicted structure was available.
