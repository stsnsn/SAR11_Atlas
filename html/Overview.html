Overview

Explore SAR11 genome, gene function, and expression in one place

The SAR11 Genome Atlas is an interactive genome and gene catalog for the SAR11 clade, the most abundant bacterial lineage in the ocean. Use the panels below to move between genome context, orthogroups, networks, literature links, and metatranscriptomic evidence.

Genome Content Viewer

Browse the genome-level catalog to inspect strain composition, gene content, and comparative context across SAR11 genomes.

OG Information Viewer

Open orthogroup-level annotations, members, and functional notes to trace how SAR11 genes are organized and annotated.

CORGIAS Network

Inspect correlation-based network structure to identify gene modules and relationships that stand out across the atlas.

SAR11 Paper Network

Connect atlas entries to the literature and curated publications so functional and evolutionary evidence stays one click away.

Synteny Network

Track conserved neighborhood patterns across genomes and explore how local gene order changes within the clade.

Metatranscriptome Viewer

Visualize expression profiles to compare SAR11 transcriptional signals across Tara Oceans metatranscriptomic datasets.

Hover or focus each panel to reveal a short description.
