Metatranscriptome viewer


Input an OG ID (e.g., OG0000173) and click the "Update Map and Correlation plots" button. You can also download the data as a CSV file by clicking the "Download CSV" button.

The plots show the correlation between the expression level of the selected OG and environmental factors.


option: Latitude, Longitude, Temperature, Sigma-theta, Salinity, Oxygen, Nitrate, Chl_a, fCDOM, Depth
