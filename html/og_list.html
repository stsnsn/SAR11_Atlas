All OG List

Browse representative KO, COG, and Pfam assignments for orthogroups in the SAR11 Genome Atlas.

OG IDs open the integrated OG Information Viewer, while KO, COG, and Pfam IDs link to their source databases. Gene-level annotations, OG HMM profiles, and OG phylogenetic trees are provided through the Download page.
